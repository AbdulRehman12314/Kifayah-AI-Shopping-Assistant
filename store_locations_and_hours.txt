COMMON DISPATCH & FACILITIES RULES
===============================================================================

1.  Dispatch slots are delivery windows, not guarantees of arrival at
    the beginning of a window.
2.  Accept express orders only when inventory, picking staff, rider
    capacity and traffic conditions support the displayed estimate.
3.  Do not accept orders outside the configured radius unless an
    authorised manager approves an exception.
4.  Store chilled and frozen goods under appropriate temperature
    controls. Follow product-specific handling requirements and keep
    temperature records.
5.  Keep raw meat/seafood separate from ready-to-eat foods and other
    goods.
6.  Record opening/closing checks, refrigeration exceptions, stock
    quarantine, dispatch delays and safety incidents.
7.  Emergency escalation contacts must be staffed and tested before
    launch. Production contact fields must never be left blank.
8.  If a hub is unavailable, disable its service area in checkout. Route
    orders to another hub only after its capacity and delivery times are
    verified.
9.  Public holidays, severe weather, road closures and security
    restrictions may require adjusted hours or temporary suspension.
10. Update the app and website whenever hours, slots, helplines or
    coverage areas change.
